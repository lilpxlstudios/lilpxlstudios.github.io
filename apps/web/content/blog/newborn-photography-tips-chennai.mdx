Bringing your newborn home is one of the most precious times in your life — and it's the perfect window to capture timeless, fine-art portraits before those tiny features change forever. Here's what we recommend to every family booking a session with us in Chennai.

## 1. Book within the first two weeks

Newborns sleep the most soundly and curl into those classic poses within their first 5–14 days. Waiting longer isn't a problem, but the earlier window makes for the calmest, most flexible session.

## 2. Keep the studio warm

Babies are most comfortable — and most likely to sleep through posing — in a warm room. Our studio in Palavakkam is kept cozy specifically for newborn sessions, so there's nothing you need to prepare there.

## 3. Feed right before the shoot

A full, sleepy baby is a cooperative baby. Plan to feed just before you arrive, or shortly after, so your little one is settled for the soft, sleepy portraits families love most.

## 4. Bring a few personal touches

A handmade blanket, a grandparent's ring, or a sentimental outfit all make lovely additions to your gallery. Let us know ahead of time and we'll build them into the shot list.

## 5. Don't stress about the "perfect" outfit

We provide a range of wraps, headbands, and props as part of every package — you're welcome to bring your own, but there's no pressure to plan an elaborate wardrobe.
