Some of our favorite maternity sessions happen right on the ECR coastline, just minutes from our studio in Palavakkam. If you're considering an outdoor session, here's what to expect.

## Best time of day

Golden hour — the hour just after sunrise or before sunset — gives the softest, warmest light for beach portraits. We'll help you pick a slot based on the season so the light works in your favor.

## What to wear

Flowing gowns in warm, muted tones photograph beautifully against the beach and sky. We keep a small collection of maternity gowns available to borrow for the session if you'd rather not buy one.

## Third trimester is ideal

Most clients book their beach session between 30–34 weeks — far enough along to show a full bump, early enough to still move comfortably across sand.

## Logistics we handle

- Scouting the exact stretch of beach based on tide and crowd levels that day
- Bringing reflectors and lighting gear to balance harsh sun if needed
- A backup indoor studio slot if weather doesn't cooperate
