About the studio

Patient hands, warm hearts, tiny humans.

Little Pixel Studios began with one belief: the first year disappears fast, and photographs are how we get to keep it. Our studio in Chennai is warm, unhurried and baby-led — feeds, naps and cuddle breaks are part of every session, never an interruption.

Every family gets their own private online gallery where you browse your photos, circle your favourites like a photographer marks a contact sheet, and we take it from there.
